Software Engineer

Design, build and maintain software systems that power the apps, tools and platforms people use every single day. The work blends creativity, logic and continuous learning.

Education B.Sc. Computer Science
Salary Range ₦6M – ₦18M / yr
Demand Very high
Time to Entry 3 – 4 years
Back to matches

Overview

Software engineers are the architects of the digital world. They design, build, test and maintain the software that powers everything from banking apps to learning platforms like EduGuide AI itself. The role rewards curiosity, structured thinking and a genuine love of problem-solving — and it's one of the few careers where continuous learning is baked into the job itself.

In Nigeria and across Africa, demand for skilled engineers is rising faster than supply. Whether you work at a startup, a bank, a global tech company or freelance, the opportunities are broad and the salaries are among the strongest in the economy.

Key Responsibilities

  • Write clean, maintainable and well-tested code
  • Design system architecture and database schemas
  • Collaborate with designers, product managers and QA engineers
  • Review code written by teammates and mentor junior engineers
  • Debug production issues and optimise performance
  • Stay current with new languages, frameworks and tools
