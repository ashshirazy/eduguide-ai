Our Story

From a simple question to a platform used across Africa

In 2023, our founding team watched bright, capable students graduate from secondary school with excellent grades — and no idea what to do next. Some chose courses because of parental pressure. Others followed trends. Very few made decisions based on who they actually were.

We knew this wasn't a talent problem. It was an information problem. So we built EduGuide AI — a platform that helps students understand themselves first, then explore careers that match their genuine interests, strengths and values.

Today, EduGuide AI serves over 50,000 students across 30+ African countries. Our recommendations are used by students, counsellors and parents alike — and every career, roadmap and resource on the platform is reviewed by education and industry experts.

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide everything we do

Our Mission

To help every student make informed, confident decisions about their education and career — using intelligent guidance that starts with who they are, not who they think they should be.

  • Personalised career guidance for every student
  • Accessible on any device, from anywhere
  • Free at the core — always

Our Vision

A continent where every student — regardless of background — can discover, pursue and succeed in careers that match their talents and passions.

  • Bridge the career-awareness gap across Africa
  • Connect learning to real opportunities
  • Build a generation of confident, purpose-driven professionals
Our Objectives

What we're here to achieve

Four concrete objectives that shape every decision, feature and partnership we make.

01

Simplify career discovery

Replace overwhelming choice with a structured, personalised assessment that surfaces the careers most likely to fit you.

02

Provide actionable roadmaps

Every career comes with a step-by-step learning roadmap — from beginner to professional — so students know exactly what to do next.
